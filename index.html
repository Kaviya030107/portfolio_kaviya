Published · ACL Anthology 2026 🏅 Ranked 12th of 22 submissions · F1 0.7959

VITECH@DravidianLangTech2026: Prompting and LoRA Adaptation for Tamil Abusive Language Detection — A Comparative Study of Open LLMs

6th Workshop on Speech, Vision, and Language Technologies for Dravidian Languages · ACL, July 2026 · pp. 436–441

Compared four open LLM families (Gemma, LLaMA, Qwen, DeepSeek-Distilled) at ~2B and 8B sizes for Tamil abusive language detection, using zero-shot, few-shot and LoRA fine-tuning. LoRA clearly beat prompting, and Gemma-2-9B + LoRA performed best.

Triambiga Krubhakaran, Senthil Kumar B, Kaviya Nagarajan, Balaji N
